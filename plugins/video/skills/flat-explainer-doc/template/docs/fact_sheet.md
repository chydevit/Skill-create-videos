# Fact sheet — The Human Era (sample)

Tags: DOCUMENTED · ARCHAEOLOGY · ESTIMATE · DEBATED · HYPOTHESIS. Every narrated claim cites a § here.

## §1 Counting years
- Most of the world uses the Gregorian calendar, which counts from Year 1 CE. There is no year 0, so 1 BCE is
  followed by 1 CE. **DOCUMENTED**
- In 1993 the geologist Cesare Emiliani proposed the Holocene / Human Era calendar. It adds 10,000 to CE years,
  so 2026 CE = 12,026 HE and 1 BCE = 10,000 HE. **DOCUMENTED** (Emiliani, *Nature* 366, 1993)

## §2 The first villages
- The Holocene, which ended the last glacial period, began about 11,700 years ago. **DOCUMENTED** (the Holocene
  GSSP, 2008)
- Göbekli Tepe, in southeastern Türkiye, has monumental enclosures from about 9500 BCE, more than 11,000 years
  ago. **ARCHAEOLOGY**
- HE year 1 falls roughly near the start of the Holocene. It is a round number, not an exact event.
  **ESTIMATE**: say "near", never "exactly".

## DO-NOT-SAY
- Do not say "Göbekli Tepe is the first city". It is not a city, and its exact function is **DEBATED**.
- Do not say "Year 1 HE is when civilization began".

## §3 Timeline props
- The Akkadian Empire under Sargon, from about 2334 BCE, is often called the first empire. Its dates are an
  **ESTIMATE**, and calling it "the first" is **DEBATED**. It is used only as a timeline marker.
